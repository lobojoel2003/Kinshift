# Code of conduct

Kinshift is built for people going through one of the hardest stretches of
family life. We want working on it to feel the opposite: welcoming, patient
and kind.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Remember that people may be sharing difficult caregiving experiences.
- Protect privacy: never share anyone's health or personal information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/lobojoel2003). Reports are reviewed promptly and
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions,
and temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break this code.

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
